Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Purpose

This is a personal, non-commercial application used by its owner to access Google Drive through the rclone software for file backup and management.

Google Drive Data

The application requests access to the owner's Google Drive files to read, upload, update and manage files as instructed by the owner.

Data Storage and Sharing

OAuth credentials are stored locally in the owner's rclone configuration. Files are transferred between the owner's computer and Google Drive. No Google Drive data is sold or shared with third parties by the application owner.

Data Retention

Files remain in Google Drive until the owner removes them. Access can be revoked through Google Account security settings.

Contact

This application is for personal use only.
